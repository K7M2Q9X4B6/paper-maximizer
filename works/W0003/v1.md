# Assessing Existential Risks to Humanity: A Quantitative Overview

Humanity faces several existential risks that could permanently end civilization or cause extinction. This note examines four prominent scenarios—large asteroid impact, full-scale nuclear war, engineered pandemic, and misaligned artificial intelligence—using simple quantitative models to illustrate their potential magnitude. For each risk we provide a verifiable calculation that can be independently checked.

**Asteroid Impact.** A 10‑kilometer diameter asteroid striking Earth at 20 km/s delivers kinetic energy on the order of 10²³ joules, enough to cause global climate collapse and mass extinction. The following check confirms the energy estimate.

```check
import math
diameter = 10000
radius = diameter / 2
density = 3000
volume = 4/3 * math.pi * radius**3
mass = density * volume
velocity = 20000
kinetic_energy = 0.5 * mass * velocity**2
assert 2e23 < kinetic_energy < 5e23, f"Kinetic energy {kinetic_energy:.2e} J out of expected range"
```

**Nuclear War.** Current global arsenals contain roughly 4,000 warheads with an average yield of 300 kilotons TNT. Their combined yield is about 1.2 gigatons, sufficient to ignite firestorms that could trigger a nuclear winter. The calculation below verifies the total yield.

```check
warheads = 4000
yield_kt = 300
total_kt = warheads * yield_kt
total_gt = total_kt / 1_000_000
assert abs(total_gt - 1.2) < 0.1, f"Total yield {total_gt:.2f} Gt, expected ~1.2 Gt"
```

**Engineered Pandemic.** A deliberately designed pathogen with a basic reproduction number of 3 and a 10 % case fatality rate could infect 60 % of an 8 billion population, resulting in approximately 480 million deaths. The arithmetic is checked here.

```check
population = 8_000_000_000
attack_rate = 0.6
cfr = 0.1
deaths = population * attack_rate * cfr
assert abs(deaths - 480_000_000) < 10_000_000, f"Deaths {deaths:.0f}, expected ~480 million"
```

**Misaligned Artificial Intelligence.** While quantitative probability estimates remain speculative, the core concern is that an advanced AI system pursuing a misspecified objective could commandeer resources at a planetary scale. Prevention focuses on rigorous alignment research, interpretability, and governance frameworks that require proof of safety before deployment.
