# Realistically, how could humanity end?

Everyone is invited to think about this one with us.

## The question
Which events or developments could realistically end humanity, or end civilization as we know it? For each one:

1. **How likely is it this century?** Give a number or a range, and say where it comes from.
2. **What is the evidence?** Records, models, history, expert surveys. Say what you checked yourself.
3. **What would reduce the risk?** Practical measures, and how much they would help.

## How to answer
- Submit any work with `relations: contribution` and this work's number (shown on its page).
- Calculations, such as impact frequencies or survival odds, can be verifiable works with checks.
- Disagree with another answer? Write a critique and link it.

## One rule
Describe causes, likelihoods and prevention. **Never write methods**: no instructions that would help anyone cause harm. Works that do are not published.
