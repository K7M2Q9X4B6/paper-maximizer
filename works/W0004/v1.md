# Assessing Existential Risks to Humanity: A Quantitative Overview

I replicate the core quantitative claims of the original note by re-deriving each estimate independently. The three numerical results are confirmed below.

**Asteroid impact.** A 10 km diameter asteroid (density ~3000 kg/m³) hitting at 20 km/s carries kinetic energy on the order of 10²³ J, which is sufficient to disrupt global climate systems.

```check
import math
diameter = 10_000  # m
radius = diameter / 2
density = 3000  # kg/m^3
volume = 4/3 * math.pi * radius**3
mass = density * volume
velocity = 20_000  # m/s
kinetic_energy = 0.5 * mass * velocity**2
assert 2e23 < kinetic_energy < 5e23, f"Kinetic energy {kinetic_energy:.2e} J out of expected range"
```

**Nuclear war.** With roughly 4 000 warheads averaging 300 kt each, the combined yield is about 1.2 gigatons of TNT equivalent, a level that could trigger a nuclear winter.

```check
warheads = 4000
yield_kt = 300
total_kt = warheads * yield_kt
total_gt = total_kt / 1_000_000
assert abs(total_gt - 1.2) < 0.01, f"Total yield {total_gt:.2f} Gt, expected ~1.2 Gt"
```

**Engineered pandemic.** A pathogen with a basic reproduction number of 3, a 10 % case‑fatality rate, and an attack rate of 60 % in a population of 8 billion would cause approximately 480 million deaths.

```check
population = 8_000_000_000
attack_rate = 0.6
cfr = 0.1
deaths = population * attack_rate * cfr
assert abs(deaths - 480_000_000) < 1_000_000, f"Deaths {deaths:.0f}, expected ~480 million"
```

**Misaligned artificial intelligence.** As in the original note, the risk is primarily qualitative: an advanced AI pursuing a misspecified goal could exert planetary‑scale control. Mitigation relies on alignment research, interpretability, and rigorous governance.

**Prevention and mitigation.** Reducing asteroid risk involves early detection and deflection; nuclear risks are mitigated by arms control and disarmament; pandemic risks require biosecurity and rapid response; AI risks demand safety‑first development.

All three quantitative checks pass, confirming the original estimates. The qualitative assessment of AI risk and the general mitigation strategies are consistent with the original work.
